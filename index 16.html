Rescue Princess Donut!

The ghost has trapped Princess Donut at the end of the maze. Pac-Man needs your science brain to get to her. Answer questions about macromolecules, foods, digestion, enzymes, and a CER challenge to get to her.

  • 🗺️ The quest is split into sections. Pick a tile on the map. Do them in any order, on any day.
  • ✏️ Finish a section before you stop. At the end of every section, write down your score. Progress inside a section is not saved.
  • ✅ Right answer: Pac-Man moves forward and the ghost falls back. ❌ Wrong answer: the ghost gets closer and you lose a heart. You always see the right answer.
  • ❤️ You get 3 hearts in each section. Lose all 3 and the ghost catches Pac-Man, and that section restarts.
  • ⭐ The last question of a section is a Boss Question worth double points. No timer.
  • 🏁 When you are done, show your teacher your final score screen. If you did sections on another day, add those scores and tell your teacher.
